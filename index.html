Injuries shape seasons, budgets, and careers. Using five seasons of injury records from the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, and Ligue 1, we test when injuries peak, which leagues carry the heaviest burden, and whether age really slows recovery.

Hypothesis 2 · Rohan Maheshwari

Which League Carries the Heaviest Injury Burden?

Premier League clubs have the highest average injury burden among Europe's top five leagues.

My visual focuses on injury burden across Europe's top five leagues. Instead of only counting the number of injuries, I looked at average games missed per club-season. That makes the view more useful because it shows how much injuries actually affected team availability over a season.

How to read this view: darker red means higher injury burden. Click a league-season cell to lock the club drilldown on the right. Hover over cells or bars to see exact values.
League-season burden heatmap and club drilldown Open full screen
Premier League stands out.
The Premier League has the highest overall injury burden across the five leagues, especially in the 2023/24 season.
La Liga is generally lower.
La Liga shows some of the lowest average games missed values, with the 2022/23 season being the lowest league-season combination in the dataset.
Club detail matters.
The drilldown shows whether a league-season value was spread across many clubs or driven by a few teams with especially high games missed.
Overall, this view supports my hypothesis that the Premier League carries the heaviest injury burden among the top five European leagues. The club drilldown adds context by showing which teams drove each league-season result, so the visual works as both a league comparison and a way to investigate specific club-level contributors.

Usability

How Testing Reshaped These Visuals

Nine usability sessions, three per member, drove the final design changes. The main focus was making interactions easier to discover, keeping values readable, and connecting high-level patterns to detailed views.

Clearer interactions.
We added stronger instructions near charts so users know when to hover, click, filter, or highlight.
More readable values.
We emphasized labels, tooltips, and spacing so users do not have to rely only on color.
Better overview-to-detail flow.
The dashboard now starts with broad patterns and uses drilldowns to explain what is driving them.
