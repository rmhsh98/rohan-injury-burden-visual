Injuries shape seasons, budgets, and careers. Using five seasons of injury records from the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, and Ligue 1, we test when injuries peak, which leagues carry the heaviest burden, and whether age really slows recovery — with an answer that surprised us.

Hypothesis 3 · Jeremy Lee

Do Older Players Really Take Longer to Recover?

In Europe's top five leagues, older players require significantly longer injury recovery times compared to younger players.

The data says the opposite. Median recovery falls as players age (r = -0.78) — and the reversal holds in all 8 injury families, all 5 leagues, and all 4 position groups. The explanation is selection: only severe youth injuries get recorded, and the veterans still playing at this level are durable survivors who get hurt more often, but for less time.

Usability

How Testing Reshaped These Visuals

Nine usability sessions (three per member) drove this design: controls were moved next to the charts they filter, visible hints now flag every interaction, clicked selections get a dark outline, and ambiguous wording ("middle 50%", arrow direction) was rewritten in plain language. The full study, task scripts, and MoSCoW-prioritized findings are in the project report.
